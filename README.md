A custom script for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III (2023) aimed towards expanding AI behavior across multiple warzone gamemodes, as well as simulating real player behavior.

# Features

**Squad bot teammates**:
- Follow you and regroup when they drift
- Actively engaging in reviving downed squadmates
- Focus fire on whoever you're fighting
- Drop spare armor plates when you're low
- Hop into your vehicle as passengers and gun down threats on wheels.

**Plunder**
- Collect cash and bank the spare right after using it on cash shops.
- Only spend on what they actually need

**All modes**
- Open loot boxes and grab plates/ammo when low
- Buy back eliminated squadmates
- Smarter fights: flanking, cover, third-partying, rotating with the circle

Performance-tuned for big lobbies. Most features can be toggled with `noice_wz_*` dvars.
